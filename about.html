Our Foundation

Transforming Healthcare Through Technology

AVANA is helping shape the next chapter of India's medical device industry by bringing globally advanced orthopaedic technologies to the Indian healthcare ecosystem, through the introduction of innovations such as Adjustable Loop TightRope® Technology, GraftLink® ACL Reconstruction Solutions, Knotless Tensionable Technology, AutoCart™ for cartilage restoration, advanced MIS technologies for Foot & Ankle, and game-changing Synergy Vision HDR Technology.

AVANA continues to bridge global innovation with Indian clinical practice, advancing surgical standards, enabling better patient outcomes, and strengthening India's position in next-generation medical technology.

Pioneering Advanced Healthcare Solutions Across India

A trusted leader in India's medical device industry, AVANA is driven by People, Performance, and Purpose.

Established in Chennai in 2009, AVANA has built a strong pan-India presence through a network of channel partners across major cities. With a team of over 350 industry professionals and an extended network of more than 200 dedicated channel partners and associates, AVANA brings together expertise, experience, and a shared commitment to advancing orthopaedic healthcare.

Over the years, AVANA has established a strong track record of sustained growth, expanding its presence, capabilities, and portfolio while continuing to earn the trust of surgeons, hospitals, and healthcare partners across India and SAARC.

Our Leadership Speaks

AVANA Medical Devices is committed to bringing advanced medical technologies, innovative therapy solutions and world-class healthcare products to India. By combining cutting-edge solutions with clinical expertise, AVANA empowers surgeons and healthcare professionals with the tools, knowledge and support needed to deliver better clinical outcomes.

Through a strong focus on innovation, continuous medical education and dedicated clinical support, AVANA works closely with healthcare professionals to enable confident decision-making and effective patient care. With a commitment to quality and excellence, the company continues to expand access to advanced solutions that address evolving clinical needs.

AVANA strives to advance orthopaedic care, improve patient outcomes and restore health, mobility and quality of life, while building long-term partnerships with surgeons, hospitals and healthcare institutions across India.
