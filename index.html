Established in 2009

Avana Medical was incorporated in Chennai in 2009 and takes its name from the Sanskrit word for preservation. We partner with orthopaedic surgeons across India, bringing them Arthrex joint preservation technology, medical education programmes and surgical support in the operating room.

We were the first organisation in India to introduce Arthrex adjustable-loop TightRope technology, the GraftLink technique for bone-conserving ACL reconstruction, and knotless repair for shoulder dislocations and rotator cuff tears.
